Real situations

What I'd do for you

Three common problems, and the steps I'd take to solve each one.

"Our app goes down at night and nobody notices until customers complain."

  1. Add health checks and metrics to each service and its database.
  2. Send alerts to Slack with the evidence attached, not just "it's down".
  3. Automate the safe fixes (restart a crashed service, reconnect a dependency) with limits and verification.
  4. Run failure drills and write runbooks for anything that needs a person.

"Our team re-types invoices and orders into spreadsheets every day."

  1. Map the manual steps and the data they touch.
  2. Build a Spring Boot API and a simple web screen to replace the spreadsheet.
  3. Add validation and duplicate or fraud checks, like InvoiceGuard.
  4. Import the old data, train the team and measure the time saved.

"We want an AI feature, but we're worried about wrong answers."

  1. Start with one narrow, high-value task such as summaries or triage.
  2. Keep AI as a labelled suggestion; rules and people approve actions.
  3. Log inputs and outputs so quality can be reviewed.
  4. Roll out to a small group first, then expand on evidence.
