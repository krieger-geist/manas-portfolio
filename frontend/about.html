About

Backend developer who cares what happens after launch.

I'm Manas Suryavanshi, a final-year Bachelor of Computer Applications student at Dezyne E'cole College. I started with Core Java and desktop apps like a bank system and a billing machine, then moved into Spring Boot, REST APIs and databases.

What hooked me was the part most tutorials skip: what happens when software breaks in production. That led to the JPMorgan Chase software engineering simulation (Kafka and Spring Boot microservices), and then to RELAY AEGIS, a self-healing system I built for Hackathon 2026 that detects failures, repairs what is safe to repair, and checks the fix before closing the incident.

I like small, careful changes, clear writing and tools that tell the truth. I'm looking for an internship or junior backend role where I can help a team ship reliable software.
